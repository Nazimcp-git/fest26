About Alert

An alert is not an alarm that ends things — it's a signal that something is beginning and worth your attention. That's the brief this year: work that notices. Paintings that catch what a hurried glance would miss. Verses written the moment a thought lands, not after it cools.

Every event on the schedule, on stage or off it, is asked to answer the same question — what does it mean to stay switched on? Alert 2k26 is this year's edition of that work: a fest run by students, for students, judged by craft and carried by four teams built from every batch on campus.

"To stay alert — is to refuse the sleepwalk. Art is our first response to the present moment."

the brief

Stay alert — to the brush before it dries, the line before it's spoken, the room before it goes quiet.

Four teams, four names

Every team was named for what it means to answer an alert — freedom, truth, effort, or connection.

01 Libera Latin for free. The team that answers an alert with unguarded, unfiltered expression. L
02 Alethia Greek for truth. The team that treats every piece as something uncovered, not invented. A
03 Opera Latin for work. The team built on rehearsal — on effort nobody sees before the curtain lifts. O
04 Relatia From relation. The team that makes art in the space between one person and another. R
